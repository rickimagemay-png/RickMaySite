Biography

A Lifetime Dedicated to Performance

Rick May was a veteran performer whose career spanned decades across stage, screen, and voice work. Beyond his iconic performances in interactive media, he served as an accomplished theater director, artistic leader, and teacher, shaping generations of dramatic talent.

Iconic Roles

The Soldier

Provided the unforgettable voice and booming presence for Team Fortress 2's iconic Soldier, delivering quotes that became central to gaming culture.

Peppy Hare

Voiced the legendary mentor figure in Star Fox 64, originating some of the most enduring phrases in video game history.

Theater & Stage

Directed and acted in hundreds of theatrical productions, including classic Shakespearean works, musicals, and contemporary drama.

His Legacy

Remembered by Millions

Rick May's work transcended standard voice acting, creating emotional connections with audiences world wide. His warmth, humor, and commanding performance style leave a permanent mark on entertainment history.
